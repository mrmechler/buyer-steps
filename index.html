You don’t need to memorize the homebuying process. Buyer Steps shows you where we are, what comes next, and what needs your attention along the way.

05
Step 05 · Showings

Tour the right homes.

We’ll schedule showings for the homes that make the cut, compare the tradeoffs, and keep refining the search as you learn what matters most in person.

06
Step 06 · Offer

Write the offer.

When you find the right home, I’ll help you evaluate the property, understand the terms, and put together an offer designed around your goals and the situation in front of us.

07
Step 07 · Due diligence

Inspect & investigate.

Once we’re under contract, the calendar starts moving. We’ll stay on top of inspections, due diligence, documents, negotiations, and the deadlines that protect your position.

08
Step 08 · Closing preparation

Loan, appraisal & title.

While you finalize the loan, the lender, title company, and I work through appraisal, title, financing conditions, and the details needed to get the transaction ready for closing.

09
Step 09 · Final walkthrough

Final walkthrough.

Before closing, we’ll walk through the property one more time to confirm its condition and verify that any agreed-upon work has been completed.

10
Step 10 · Closing

Close on your home.

You’ll review the final figures, sign your closing documents, and complete the remaining steps required for the transaction to fund and close.

11
Step 11 · Keys

Welcome home.

Once the transaction has closed and possession is delivered under the contract, the home is yours. Time to get the keys and start the next chapter.
